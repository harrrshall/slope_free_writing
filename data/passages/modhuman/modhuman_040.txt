He was already out of his pilot s chair and pulling himself to the back of the little wrecker. The ship he owned was almost all cargo bay, but he did have all four sleep pods required by law nestled in the corridor to the main airlock. He punched in the code sequence for sleep release and waited. If he hadn t been in zero gravity he would have bounced from foot to foot, but instead he fidgeted by pushing himself back and forth between the corridor walls. At long last the pod door slid aside, and Jade floated out, her short black hair standing on end. What ve you got? she asked at once. Big one, he said. 100km. She gave a small smile. We d better get to work. The wreck wasn t just large, they soon discovered. It was a transport ship hauling platinum, and Ben was a little surprised the company hadn t sent out a recovery team.
