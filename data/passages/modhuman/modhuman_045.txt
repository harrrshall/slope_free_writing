The stall, with its torn black cover and rundown tables, was located in an alley adjacent to the marketplace. A sign hung from one of the beams, the words carelessly engraved on a metal plate: Used Robot Parts. The display did nothing to attract customers. Bolts, wires and circuits were scattered across the counters, watched over by a shabby-looking youth. He watched the passersby disinterestedly, and met enquiries into his wares with indifference bordering on rudeness. A tall elderly man approached the stall. Do you have Iridian Processing Boards? he asked in a low voice. The youth jerked his head in assent. Specifications? Name? Number? Give me The man scratched his white beard. Something that begins with 021. I want to know their occupation before I purchase. The youth typed some words into a round tablet that he held in a pouch around his neck. 021339, a botanist. Or 021999, a consultant for RemTech.
