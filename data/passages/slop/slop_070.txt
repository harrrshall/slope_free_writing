The sun was setting over the vast, shimmering expanse of the ocean, casting a golden hue over the deck of the small yacht. The sea breeze carried the salty air and the distant sounds of seagulls and the occasional splash of a jumping fish. On the deck, a group of five friends sat around a small table, each one clutching a map with the same wide-eyed excitement. Among them, Alex, the self-proclaimed navigator, was pointing out various points on the map. According to this, we're just north of the Azores. We should be able to make it to Bermuda in a week if we stay on course. Maggie, a marine biologist and Alex's girlfriend, nodded enthusiastically. And then we'll get to study the coral reefs and the diversity of marine life. I've never seen anything like it. Next to her, Tom, the electronics expert, was fiddling with a new satellite phone he had installed just for this trip.
