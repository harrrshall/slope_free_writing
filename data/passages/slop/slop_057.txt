As the days passed, Eamon's stay became a source of tension in Mossgrove. The villagers, enchanted by his stories, began to question their lord's strict rules. They saw the kindness in Isolde's eyes when she spoke with Eamon, a kindness they had never seen in their stern lord. One evening, as the sun dipped below the horizon, Eamon shared a story of a realm where music could heal the wounded and love could bridge even the deepest of divides. His voice, rich with emotion, echoed through the village. Isolde listened, her heart swelling with a mix of joy and sorrow. She realized then that she had fallen for this stranger, his spirit as wild and free as the wind. That night, under the starlit sky, Isolde and Eamon confessed their love for each other. They knew their union was forbidden, but they couldn't deny the pull between them. Lord Edgar, witnessing their embrace, was a silent observer, his heart aching with a love he had never expressed. The next morning, Eamon prepared to leave. The villagers, moved by his stories and the love they had witnessed, begged Lord Edgar to let Eamon stay.
