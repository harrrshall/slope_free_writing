Jericho rode into Redemption silhouetted against the bruised purple of the setting sun, a lone figure astride a cybernetically enhanced mustang. His duster billowed like a storm cloud, concealing the details of his gear, but the glint of chrome and the hum of hidden servos hinted at the technology Cletus had whispered about. He dismounted with a practiced grace, his boots crunching on the sun-baked dust, and surveyed the ramshackle town with eyes as sharp as a hawk's. He walked towards the saloon, his boots echoing on the warped wooden planks, and Cletus, who had been watching from the doorway, stepped forward. "Jericho, I presume?" "The one and only," Jericho replied, his voice a low baritone, tinged with a faint, almost musical, electronic modulation. He offered a hand, calloused and strong, and Cletus shook it, surprised by the warmth that radiated from the cybernetic grip. "Doc Petrova," Cletus gestured towards the saloon, "she's been expectin' ya." Anya emerged, her silver braid catching the last rays of sunlight. Jericho's gaze lingered on her for a moment, a flicker of something unreadable in his eyes before he inclined his head in a curt nod. "Dr. Petrova," he acknowledged, his voice carrying a hint of respect. "Heard tales of your skill. Redemption's fortunate to have you." He led them to a battered wagon, its canvas cover pulled back to reveal a workbench laden with tools and components that gleamed with an otherworldly sheen. Anya recognized some of the materials – salvaged circuitry, repurposed alloys, and strange, luminescent crystals – remnants of a technology long forgotten by the rest of the world.
