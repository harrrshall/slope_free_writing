In the heart of Florence, where the sun painted the cobblestones with a golden hue, there lived a man named Lorenzo. He was a skilled artisan, a master of glass, renowned for his intricate creations that adorned the homes of the wealthy and the sacred spaces of the city. Yet, Lorenzo's heart harbored a secret, a darkness that gnawed at him like a relentless beast. Lorenzo's workshop was a labyrinth of color and light, filled with the hum of his labor and the scent of molten glass. But it was also a place where shadows danced, and whispers of a different sort echoed through the night. For Lorenzo was a thief, a master of disguise and deception, stealing from the rich to fuel his art and his vice. One evening, as the city's bells tolled the hour of vespers, Lorenzo donned his cloak of darkness and ventured into the night. His target was the Palazzo Medici, where the wealthy merchant, Giovanni de' Medici, kept his treasures. Lorenzo's eyes gleamed with anticipation as he scaled the walls, his fingers finding purchase on the cold stone. Inside, the palazzo was a symphony of opulence, but Lorenzo's heart was a drumbeat of focus. He moved through the shadows, his breath steady, his senses heightened. He found the study, a room filled with books, maps, and the gleam of gold. The Medici family was known for their wealth and their patronage of the arts, but Lorenzo was not there for their art. He was there for their gold. As he reached for a heavy chest, he heard a noise.
