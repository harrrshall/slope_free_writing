The sun dipped low, casting elongated shadows that danced with the dust kicked up by the mag-lev carriage as it rumbled down the dusty thoroughfare. The thoroughfare was called Duster's Row, a name left over from a time when the ground was traversable by wheel, before the magnets had replaced the horse. The carriages moved like ghosts through a desert town, through the dusty remnants of the frontier town. A tall figure sat at the end of the bar, the lone human in a room filled with androids. He was the new lawman of Iron Springs. His name was Jack. He could pass for the same gunslingers who once roamed this land, if not for the glowing blue LED lights that ran from his neck to his fingertips. The glowing lights had been put there as a sign of the times. His artificial enhancements were necessary to his work. In fact, his eyes were glowing, and their blue glow was one of his most intimidating features. His hands were wrapped around a glass filled with a dark brown whiskey, but his attention was focused on the holopaper. The images from his local news source shimmered into view as he swiped his hands through the holographic projections. He was looking at pictures of the men he was supposed to arrest.
