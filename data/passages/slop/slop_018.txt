The rain had stopped, the sky now a pale gray, a stark contrast to the darkness that enveloped the warehouse. Detective John Walker and his partner, Sarah, stood in the middle of the now-quiet street, their hearts heavy with the weight of their discovery. John's gaze swept the empty warehouse, his mind reeling. "They're all gone," he said, his voice hoarse and raw. "Every last one." Sarah nodded, her eyes red and dilated, a mix of shock and horror lavishing her features. "I saw them, too. But why? What could have done this?" The answer to that question eluded them, and the mystery would haunt their dreams. As they turned to leave, their footsteps slow and cautious, the sun peered out from behind the clouds, casting a strange light on the scene. The warehouse, once a place of darkness and horror, now seemed almost peaceful, a silent sentinel watching over the city. Little did they know, the true horror was only beginning. As they descended the street, their minds racing with questions and fears, a strange, sinister laugh echoed in their ears, a harbinger of the true evil that lurked in the city's shadows.
