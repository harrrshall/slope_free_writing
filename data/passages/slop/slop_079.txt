The village of Ashwood lay nestled in a valley, its people living in a world of stark contrasts - the lush green forests and sparkling streams that surrounded them, and the dark, foreboding castle that loomed overhead. It was said that the castle was home to the tyrannical Lord Ravenswood, a man feared by all who lived in the village below. Ava was a young woman with raven-black hair and piercing green eyes, her beauty and spirit striking a chord in the hearts of all who knew her. She was the daughter of the village elder, a man named Eryndor who had once been a close friend of Lord Ravenswood. The two had been inseparable, their bond forged in the fires of battle and their loyalty to one another unshakeable. But times had changed, and the friendship between Eryndor and Ravenswood had soured. The lord had grown increasingly cruel, his rule marked by fear and oppression. Eryndor, once his most trusted advisor, now found himself at odds with Ravenswood's brutal policies. Ava's life was one of quiet desperation, her days spent helping her father in his work as the village elder. But her nights were filled with the sound of whispers, the villagers speaking in hushed tones of a plan to overthrow Ravenswood and bring peace to their troubled land.
