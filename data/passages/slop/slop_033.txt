In the heart of the medieval countryside, a small village named Valoria nestled beside a serene lake. It was the village where everyone knew everyone’s business, and the grapevine was as thick as the trees that lined the lake’s shores. The village had its own share of drama, but none quite like the story of Mireille and Luc. Mireille was the village blacksmith’s daughter, known for her strong hands and fiery spirit. Luc was the young squire, destined to become the next lord of the neighboring estate, the manor of Arden. They had grown up together, playmates in a world of mud pies and toy swords. As the years passed, their friendship blossomed into a deep and cherished love. It was a crisp autumn morning, the leaves turning from green to gold, when a royal messenger arrived in Valoria. He brought news that had the entire village buzzing like bees in a disturbed hive. The king had decreed that all eligible bachelors must participate in a tournament to win the hand of Princess Isolde, who would soon be of marrying age. The tournament would be held in the grand castle at the heart of the kingdom, and the winner would not only earn the princess’s hand but also a title and a substantial dowry. Luc, as expected, was one of the many who signed up for the tournament.
