The year was 1924, and the world had changed. The Great War had ended, but the true conflict was only beginning. The cities were no longer filled with the hustle and bustle of people going about their daily lives, but instead, they were filled with the constant hum of machines and the occasional siren of the Enforcers. The Enforcers were the new law enforcement, tasked with maintaining order in a world where the government had taken control. They were equipped with the latest technology and had the power to arrest or even execute anyone who dared to step out of line. Amelia had always been one to question the status quo. She had seen the world before the war, and she knew what it was like to live in a world without fear. But now, she was just trying to survive in the new world. She worked at the factory, putting in long hours every day, creating the very machines that kept the city running.
