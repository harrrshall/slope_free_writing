As she settled back into her apartment, a wave of unease still lingered from her day at Dreamcatchers. Rachel tried to shake it off, reflecting on the stories and struggles that seeped into her work from the lives she touched each day. However, her thoughts kept drifting back to the once-promising community center now standing as a hollow shell. Her gaze wandered to the dimly lit photo of Mark on her nightstand. It was taken a decade ago, when they both roamed New York, embarking on midnight escapades, chasing late-night jazz sessions, and escaping exhaustion by 6 a.m. sunrises at Washington Square Park. Mark's infectious grin and wide eyes always found a way to transport her back to a simpler time when the world was brimming with vibrancy. Rachel let go of the last remnants of her day, trying to momentarily escape the suffocating air of neglect. As she rubbed the corners of the worn frame to coax it back to life, her thoughts slipped onto the community she once grew up with, its struggles and brokenness laid stark before her eyes. Where did the Dreamcatchers community center find its way from its gleaming vision to a vacant, empty shell? Just as she finally let her thoughts release, Mark's message stirred a gentle jolt in her chest. Her eyes broke from the faded memory on her nightstand and fell upon a scribbled phrase, 'Join me for a night out this weekend - will explain later.'
