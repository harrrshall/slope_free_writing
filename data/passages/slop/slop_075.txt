The cobblestones of Castlehaven were slick with rain, reflecting the flickering gaslight in a shimmering mosaic. Lady Elara, draped in emerald silk that clung to her curves despite the damp chill, hurried through the shadowed alleys, her heart a frantic drum against her ribs. Her silk slippers whispered against the wet stones, the only sound besides the distant rumble of thunder. She reached the blacksmith's forge, its doorway a gaping maw spewing smoke and sparks. Inside, the air was thick with the scent of iron and sweat, the rhythmic clang of hammer against steel a familiar lullaby. A hulking figure, his face etched with soot and sweat, stood hunched over a roaring furnace, oblivious to her arrival. "Master Thorne," Elara hissed, her voice barely audible above the din. Thorne straightened, his calloused hand wiping the grime from his brow. Recognition dawned in his weathered eyes, widening in surprise. "Lady Elara? What brings you to this humble place at this hour?" "I need your help," she pleaded, her voice trembling. "My husband, Lord Alaric, he... he's in danger." Thorne's brows furrowed. "Danger? From whom?" Elara hesitated, fear constricting her throat. "From the Duke. He's... he's plotting against Alaric, seeking to usurp his lands." Thorne's grip tightened on his hammer, the metal handle creaking. "The Duke? That viper! He's been eyeing Alaric's holdings for months now, always looking for a weakness." He glanced at the flames dancing in the forge, their light reflecting in his steely gaze.
