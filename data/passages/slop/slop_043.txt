She walked towards the town, ready to share her findings with the mayor, ready to start the process of restoring the mill. But as she reached the town's outskirts, she paused, a sudden chill running down her spine. She turned around, her eyes scanning the mill's facade. There, in one of the windows, she thought she saw a figure. A small girl, her hair wild, her eyes wide with terror. Amelia's heart skipped a beat. Could it be? Could there be another trapped soul in the mill? She took a deep breath, her resolve strengthening. She had come to Hollow Creek to lay the mill's ghosts to rest. She had helped one soul find its way. She would not stop now. She would do whatever it took to help any other lost souls find their way. She turned back towards the mill, her steps steady, her heart filled with determination. The mill's journey was not over yet. But she was ready. She was always ready to help.
