It was a drizzly evening in the sweltering summer of 1922, and the streets of New Orleans were alive with jazz music and the sweet scent of magnolias. The city was abuzz with the latest speakeasy, a secret hideaway where the city's elite gathered to indulge in bootlegged cocktails and the hottest jazz bands. But for our protagonist, Jack "The Ace" Anderson, the city was a stage, and he was the star. Jack was a struggling comedian, working multiple gigs a night to make ends meet, but his dreams of stardom were within reach. He had a quick wit, a charming smile, and a knack for making people laugh. But, his biggest problem was his tendency to get himself into trouble. Whether it was accidentally setting his own pants on fire during a performance or getting into a barroom brawl with a rival comedian, Jack's antics were always making headlines in the local papers. One fateful evening, Jack received an invitation from the enigmatic and beautiful, Lola LaRue, to perform at her exclusive speakeasy, "Lola's Lounge." The catch? He had to perform a special set for a mysterious and reclusive millionaire, Mr. Edward Blackstone, who was rumored to be a notorious miser. The pay was astronomical, and Jack couldn't resist the temptation. As Jack arrived at Lola's Lounge, he was greeted by the sultry sounds of a saxophone and the flashing lights of the speakeasy's interior. The air was thick with cigarette smoke and the hum of conversation. Jack spotted Lola herself, resplendent in a beaded flapper dress, sipping a cocktail at the bar. She blew him a kiss and whispered, "You're on in five, darling." As Jack took the stage, he surveyed the crowd, his eyes locking onto the figure of Mr. Blackstone, who sat in the back, shrouded in shadows.
