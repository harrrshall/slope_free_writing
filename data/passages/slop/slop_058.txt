The air crackled with unseen energy, driving Lily back against the wall. She braced herself, her mind racing, trying to make sense of the ghostly apparitions that surrounded her. The old man, the girl, and now this—a creature that seemed to be a living embodiment of decay and despair. "I...I came to uncover the truth," Lily stammered, her voice steady despite her fear. "I didn't mean to wake you." The spectral woman snarled, her form writhing like a slithering snake. "The truth will destroy you, just as it destroyed them. You are the last, the one who will set us free—or so we are told." Lily's hand tightened around the silver crescent moon pendant. It glowed faintly, a whisper of light in the chaos. "I won't let that happen." The old man coughed, a sound that sent shivers down her spine. "You must. It's your destiny. But be warned—you can't outrun this. It's within you, too." His words, though murmured, held a weight that sent a chill down her spine. Suddenly, the room spun, and Lily stumbled, seeing double. When her vision cleared, the figures were gone. She took a deep breath, forcing herself to calm. The door, she realized, was still locked. She placed her hand firmly against it, feeling for any give. A surge of energy coursed through her, and the lock snapped open with a sickening screech. Stepping out into the rain-soaked corridor, she turned, gazing back at the abandoned ward. "I won't let this place win," she whispered, her voice echoing off the cold walls.
