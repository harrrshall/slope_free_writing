The dimly lit hallway stretched out before them, the floorboards creaking under their feet as they moved deeper into the house. James's lantern flickered, casting long shadows that seemed to dance on the walls, and Sarah's flashlight beam cut through the darkness, illuminating the path ahead. As they reached the grand staircase, James stopped and turned to Sarah with a serious expression. Do you want to explore the upper floors? he asked. There are stories of strange happenings up there, and I'd rather not do it alone. Sarah nodded, her heart pounding in her chest, but her curiosity and courage reignited. I'm with you, she said firmly. Together, they ascended the stairs, the air growing colder with each step. When they reached the second floor, a chilling breeze swept past them, carrying with it a faint, whispering sound. Sarah paused, her hand reaching for James's shoulder, her eyes wide with apprehension. What was that? she whispered, her voice barely audible above the wind. James tightened his grip on the lantern, his eyes narrowing as he listened intently. I think it's coming from the west wing, he replied, his voice steady despite the unease he felt.
