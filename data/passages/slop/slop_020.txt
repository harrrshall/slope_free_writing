As their romance blossomed, they found ways to steal moments together. They shared intimate dinners by the candlelight, and James gifted Elizabeth with a painting of her favorite flower, a wild rose, growing on the side of an old wall. Their love became a secret sanctuary in the midst of the bustling city, a place where they found solace and joy. However, their happiness was short-lived. The factory owner, Mr. Carter, learned of their forbidden romance. He did not approve of his employees' relationships, and his anger knew no bounds. He banned them from the factory, threatening to fire them both if their tryst continued. Heartbroken, Elizabeth and James faced a difficult choice. They knew their love was not wrong, but they also knew the consequences of defying Mr. Carter. In the end, they made a daring decision—they ran away. With a few belongings and their artwork, they fled to a small cottage on the other side of the city, a place where no one knew them. There, they built a life together, painting, creating, and dreaming. Their art became a testament to their love, a way to support themselves, and a way to bring beauty to the world, no matter the cost.
