A wave of energy surged from the book, pushing back against the Lupa's menacing presence. The alleyway pulsed with an ethereal light, casting dancing shadows that seemed to writhe with malevolent intent. The creature recoiled, its amber eyes widening with a flicker of fear. Isabella held the book aloft, her voice gaining strength with each spoken syllable. The air thrummed with a resonant hum, an unseen force coalescing around her. The Lupa snarled, a guttural sound ripped from its throat, then turned, dissolving into the shadows with a final, frustrated whimper. The oppressive weight that had pressed down on Isabella's chest lifted, replaced by a residual tingling that spoke of power unleashed. She lowered the book, its leather cover still warm against her palm. The alleyway fell silent, the shadows retreating back into their familiar stillness. The only sound was the gentle whisper of the wind rustling through the eaves above. Isabella leaned against the cool stone, catching her breath, her heart still pounding from the encounter. The fear hadn't vanished entirely, but it was tempered now by a newfound resolve. She was alone, but she was not powerless. Her father's knowledge, the ancient wisdom she'd inherited, was a shield, a weapon, a legacy she would carry forward. As the first sliver of dawn peeked over the rooftops, bathing the city in a soft, ethereal light, Isabella straightened, a new purpose hardening her gaze.
