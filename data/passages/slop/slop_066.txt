The year was 1875, and the city of Manchester was a bustling hub of the Industrial Revolution. Smokestacks rose into the smoggy sky, belching out thick clouds of coal dust and steam. Amidst this chaos, a young woman named Eliza worked in the textile mills. Her nimble fingers danced across the loom, weaving intricate patterns into the cotton fabric. Eliza was not just a weaver; she was a weaver with a secret. She could weave magic into the fabric of her creations. It was a gift passed down through generations in her family, a gift that had been hidden and suppressed for years. But Eliza felt the pull of her gift growing stronger with each passing day. The fabric she wove became imbued with a life of its own, glowing with an ethereal light that only she could see. One evening, as the sun set and the city lights flickered to life, Eliza felt a strange presence in the air. It was a whisper of magic, a call to her gift. She left the factory early, weaving her way through the crowded streets until she reached the old mill by the river. There, she found an old man, hunched over a small, flickering flame.
