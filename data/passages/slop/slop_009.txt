The sun had dipped below the horizon by the time the cardinal's men reached the old cathedral. Their journey had been fraught with uncertainty, but Isabella's words had guided them true. The stone structure loomed before them, its ancient spires reaching for the night sky like the fingers of a long-dead giant. The three men, armed with swords and a growing sense of unease, approached the entrance. According to Isabella's map, the artifact was sealed behind a series of intricate mechanisms, designed to deter the unworthy. They knew they had little time; the night's shadows were growing longer, and the sorceress's warning of an ancient guardian still rang in their ears. The first attempt to unlock the secrets of the cathedral's heart failed. The mechanism was complex, a dance of cogs and gears, and their efforts only served to trigger a series of loud, echoing chimes. The men exchanged frustrated glances, their initial confidence wavering. But a surge of determination coursed through them, fueled by their desire to protect the Vatican and uncover the truth. They divided, each taking a different approach, determined to solve the riddle that lay before them. As they worked, a chill swept through the cathedral, causing the men to pause. In that moment, a figure emerged from the shadows, its form translucent yet menacing. The guardian, long dormant, had awoken. The men drew their swords, but their weapons seemed insignificant against the creature's otherworldly power.
