As the night drew to a close, Publius stood amidst the supernaturals, his mind reeling from the events that had transpired. The dragon, the nymphs, the satyrs, the fauns - all had come together in a display of power and majesty that left Publius breathless. He looked around at his friends, Gaius, Marcus, and the others, their faces aglow with wonder and amazement. They had borne witness to the supernatural world, the one that lurked just beneath the surface of their reality. The dragon stepped forward, its eyes glowing in the moonlight. "This is just the beginning, Publius," it said, its voice softening. "There is a world beyond what you know, a world of magic and wonder. We invite you to join us, to walk beside us as we navigate the tides of this new era." Publius looked at his friends, at the supernaturals around him, and knew that he could not turn back now. He had been given a glimpse into a world that few had ever seen, and he could not unsee it. He nodded, his heart filled with a mix of fear and excitement. "I accept," he said, his voice steady. "I am ready to see what lies beyond." The dragon smiled, its sharp teeth glinting in the moonlight. "Then let us begin," it said, "Let us show you the world as it truly is." And with that, the supernaturals began to disperse, melting back into the shadows from which they came. Publius watched as they went, his mind buzzing with the possibilities of what lay ahead. As he turned to leave, Gaius caught his arm, his eyes alight with excitement. "This is just the beginning," he said, grinning, "The night has only just begun."
