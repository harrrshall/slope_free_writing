In the heart of the mill, the spectral light grew brighter, its cold glow casting eerie shadows on the ancient walls. The voice that had first whispered now boomed, echoing through the vast spaces, "You have found me, intruders. Now, you shall pay the price." Lucas and Amelia, hands still clasped, stood frozen in terror. The cold seemed to seep into their bones, their breath misting in the frigid air. They could feel the malevolent presence growing stronger, the mill's darkness pulsating around them. Suddenly, Lucas remembered the old tales, the rumors of Elijah Blackwood's pact with darkness. "We need to leave," he whispered, his voice barely audible. "We need to find a way out." Amelia nodded, her eyes wide with fear. They turned, their hearts pounding, and began to run. The mill's vast interior seemed to stretch out before them, the shadows dancing and twisting, mocking their desperation. As they ran, the voice followed them, its tone now mocking, "You cannot escape, fools. You have trespassed in my domain, and now, you shall join me in eternal darkness." They stumbled, their breaths coming in ragged gasps, their hearts pounding like drums in their ears. Then, they saw it - a faint sliver of moonlight, streaming through a narrow opening. Hope surged through them, and with a final burst of speed, they rushed towards it. As they reached the opening, a cold, bony hand reached out from the shadows, its fingers brushing against Amelia's arm. She screamed, a sound that echoed through the mill, a sound of pure terror. Lucas, his heart pounding, pulled her through the opening, and they tumbled out into the cool night air. They found themselves in a small clearing, the mill looming behind them like a monstrous beast.
