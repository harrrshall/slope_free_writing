The flickering gaslight cast long, skeletal shadows across the cobblestone street, dancing in time with the rhythmic clip-clop of Lorenzo's horse-drawn cart. He whistled a jaunty tune, oblivious to the pair of men trailing him from the gloom. Their faces, obscured by the darkness, were set in grim determination. Lorenzo was a wealthy merchant, notorious for his opulent displays of gold and jewels, and tonight, he was carrying a particularly valuable prize: a ruby necklace, the centerpiece of his collection, rumored to have been worn by a Medici queen. The men, Bruno and Marco, were desperate. Their families had fallen on hard times, and the whispers of Lorenzo's riches had turned into an obsession. Tonight, they were going to take what they felt was rightfully theirs. They followed Lorenzo to his sprawling villa, a monument to his success, nestled within the heart of Florence. The guards, two burly men with swords strapped to their hips, stood stoically at the gate, their faces illuminated by the flickering light of a lantern. Hold, Bruno hissed to Marco. He stepped forward, his voice surprisingly calm despite the tremor in his hands.
