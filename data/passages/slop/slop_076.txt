The streets of downtown Los Angeles seemed to stretch on forever, a seemingly endless expanse of concrete and steel that swallowed Ava and Max whole. They walked in silence, the only sound the soft hum of the city's pulse beating around them. Ava's camera hung heavy around her neck, a constant reminder of the world she loved and the mystery that now surrounded her. As they turned a corner, the towering skyscrapers gave way to a narrow alleyway, the air thick with the smells of trash and exhaust fumes. Ava wrinkled her nose in distaste, but Max led her deeper into the alley, his eyes fixed on some point ahead. What is this place, Max? Ava asked, her voice barely above a whisper. Max didn't answer, his gaze fixed on a door at the end of the alley. It was an unassuming entrance, a plain metal door with a small sign that read Eclipse Investigations. Ava's eyes narrowed, her mind racing with possibilities. As they approached the door, it swung open, revealing a dimly lit room filled with rows of computer screens and dusty files. Ava's eyes widened as she took in the scene before her, a sense of unease creeping up her spine. Welcome to the heart of the city's underbelly, Max said, a small smile playing on his lips.
